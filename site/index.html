Originals at home.
Compact copies on your iPhone.

Homespun keeps full-quality photos and videos on a computer you own, and puts small, good-looking copies back in Apple Photos.

In testing on TestFlight. Not yet on the App Store.

How it works

  1. Back up the photos and videos you choose. Each original goes to your server, which checks it byte for byte (SHA-256) before anything else happens.
  2. Get compact copies back in Apple Photos with the same date, place, and favorites. Live Photos stay live.
  3. Compare, then free up space. Look at the original and its compact copy side by side, zoom in on both at once, and keep any original you'd rather have full size on your iPhone. Homespun asks iOS to remove an original only after your server proves it holds an identical copy. iOS asks you to confirm, and the item stays in Recently Deleted.
  4. Restore an original whenever you want it back. Homespun brings it home from your server, checks it, puts it in Photos with its favorite and albums, and only then removes the compact copy.

On its own, if you like

Automatic Mode backs up new photos and videos overnight while your iPhone charges. You choose when they get compact copies: right away, or once they're a week to three months old, so the photos you're still sorting through stay full size. Favorites keep their originals, and anything you delete on your iPhone before then is never sent back. Cloud Copies will also copy each original to a cloud service, starting with OneDrive, as one more backup.

Where your originals live

A Mac

Any Mac that stays on. It makes every compact copy itself, Live Photos included. Set it up.

Linux or a NAS

A Linux computer, or a NAS with Docker (Synology, QNAP, UGREEN, TrueNAS, Unraid, and others). Set up Linux or a NAS.

With Immich

Already back up to Immich? Homespun uses the originals there, checks them byte for byte, and never changes them. Connect it.

Made for every iPhone

Including iPhone Duo: folded it works like any iPhone; open, your library, the comparison of an original with its copy, and the settings use the whole display.

Free, with one purchase for automation

Backing up, compact copies, comparing, freeing up space, and restoring originals are free. A one-time purchase unlocks Automatic Mode and Cloud Copies together, on all your devices with your Apple Account.

Open source

The app and the server are on GitHub: the app under Apache-2.0, the server under AGPL-3.0. You can read every line that touches your photos, and build it yourself.

Why

Homespun grew out of a personal setup that shrank a 37,000-item photo library to about a fifth of its size while keeping every original safe at home. Photos fit within 2048 pixels, videos within 720p, and nothing is deleted until a verified copy exists.
