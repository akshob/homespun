Privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

In short: Your photos and videos stay on your iPhone or go to a server that you run. The developer of Homespun never receives them. The app has no accounts, ads, or tracking, and it never sells or shares your data. If you choose to share analytics, the developer receives anonymous usage counts and crash reports; if you send a problem report, the developer receives what you write.

Your photo library

With your permission, the Homespun app reads the photos and videos you choose and saves compact copies to an album named "Homespun" in your library. It never changes your originals. It asks iOS to delete an original only when you choose to, and only after your server has proven it holds an identical copy. iOS asks you to confirm every deletion, and deleted items stay in Recently Deleted.

Your own server

If you connect Homespun to a server you run, such as Homespun Server or an Immich server, the app sends your originals to that server (or lets it find them in your Immich library) and downloads compact copies from it. The app connects to Homespun Server over an encrypted connection and keeps its key fingerprint with the pairing token in the Keychain, used only to talk to your server. The server's address and Bonjour service name stay on your iPhone. If its address changes, the app checks the pinned key before sending its token to the new address. A random installation ID and the last device name your server confirmed are also kept in the device-only, non-synced Keychain. They survive unpairing or reinstalling the app, but not erasing the phone. Pairing sends them to your server so an active device record can be reclaimed after your approval; the server stores only a hash of the ID. This ID is never included in analytics or problem reports and is separate from the optional analytics identifier. An Immich API key, if you use one, stays on your server. The developer of Homespun has no access to your server or its contents.

Local Network access finds Homespun Server using Bonjour (_homespun._tcp only) and connects to it. Camera access is used only to scan a pairing QR code; camera images are not stored or sent.

If you separately configure a cloud connector on your server, that server sends originals to the service you choose. OneDrive sign-in uses a code on Microsoft's website; its tokens stay on your server. The developer receives no originals or sign-in details. These connectors are configured separately on the server, not in the 1.0.0 app.

Analytics you choose to share

Homespun asks once, during setup, whether you want to share anonymous analytics with its developer, and you can change your answer any time in the app under Server › Share Analytics. Nothing is sent unless you choose Share Analytics. If you do, the app sends:

  • Usage counts, with numbers rounded: which setup steps you went through, whether pairing worked, your server's kind and version, how many items a backup or Free Up Space handled and roughly how large they were, which features were used, and short error codes.
  • Crash and performance reports that iOS gives the app (MetricKit), such as where it crashed or hung and how long it took to start. iOS provides these only if Share With App Developers is on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.
  • The app's version, the iPhone model and iOS version, and a random identifier that's replaced whenever you stop sharing, so data from before and after can't be connected.

Analytics never include photos, videos, file names, places, album names, your server's name or address, or anything else that identifies you. They're used only to find problems and improve Homespun, never for advertising, and never combined with data from other companies.

Problem reports

When you use Report a Problem (Server › Help and About), the app sends what you write, the email address you give (optional, used only to reply), and, unless you turn it off, diagnostics you can read before sending: the app and iOS versions, item counts, your settings, your server's kind, version and check results, a summary of recent backups, the app's log from that session, and iOS's latest crash report. Like analytics, they never include photos, file names, or your server's name or address. Reports are sent whether or not you share analytics, because you send them on purpose.

A report without an email includes your analytics install identifier only while you share analytics. A report with an email never includes that identifier, so it cannot link your analytics to your reply address. The email, message, and any included crash or diagnostic data can be linked to you; analytics, performance data and device identifiers are not linked to your identity.

The demo

Try the Demo uses sample photos that Homespun draws on your iPhone (and a few videos that come with the app) and a pretend server inside the app. For App Review only, entering a review code instead downloads 16 sample photos from the developer's private Cloudflare storage, with progress and SHA-256 integrity checks. The review code and your IP address reach the developer's Cloudflare service to authorize the download. Requests carry no installation, analytics, device, or photo-library identifiers, cookies, or app version. A short-lived access token authorizes only the sample files. Neither the code, token nor raw IP address is stored by the developer; only aggregate usage counters and short-lived keyed rate-limit counters are kept. Request logging is disabled. The code is kept in this iPhone's non-synced Keychain until you clear it. Downloads are repeated after each cold launch, not after backgrounding, and failure uses drawn photos instead. All prepared sample photos are deleted the next time Homespun starts. In the demo, Homespun doesn't read or change your Photos library, records no analytics, sends no problem reports, and pairs with no real photo-backup server. Leaving deletes the demo's settings and records; samples stay until the next launch. Your own server's settings and records stay unchanged.

Where it goes and how long it's kept

Analytics, crash reports, and problem reports go to a service the developer runs on Cloudflare (telemetry.homespun.photos), and problem reports are also emailed to the developer. Cloudflare processes this data on the developer's behalf, under obligations that provide the same protection described here. Usage counts are kept for about 13 months, performance reports for 3 months, crash reports for 12 months, and problem reports for 2 years, then deleted. To delete shared analytics and reports associated with an install identifier sooner, open Server › Share Analytics in the app and choose Delete Shared Data. Reports with an email have no install identifier: to delete one, email support@homespun.photos with its report number. You can also use that address to request deletion of any report.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, or third-party scripts. It is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site, as described in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Purchases

Version 1.0.0 has no in-app purchases and sends no purchase events. Its privacy manifest conservatively retains a purchase-history declaration for code reserved for a later version. If purchases become available, this policy will describe their handling before that version ships.
